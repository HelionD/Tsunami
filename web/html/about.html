About Tsunami

An open-source chaos engineering tool. Break your system on your terms — before production does it for you.

Our Mission

Break things before they break you

Tsunami is an open-source chaos engineering tool for anyone responsible for keeping systems reliable — from developers to SREs, and everyone in between. No enterprise pricing, no black-box tooling — just raw, controlled chaos you can understand, fork, and improve.

CPU Spike Simulation

Intentionally spike CPU usage on target processes or Kubernetes pods to see how your application handles high compute pressure. Configurable intensity and duration.

Fault Tolerance Testing

Trigger controlled failures and observe how your system recovers. Test circuit breakers, retry logic, and fallback mechanisms under real stress conditions.

v1 ✓

Stress Testing

Push memory and resource limits to their edges. Identify breaking points before they surface in production where it actually costs you.

v1 ✓

Kubernetes Pod Stressing

Target specific pods inside your Kubernetes cluster. Stress individual services without taking down your whole environment.
